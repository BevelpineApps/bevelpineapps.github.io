# Data Processing Addendum — Bevelpine Apps

**Last updated:** 15 September 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") forms part of the agreement under which a customer
("**Customer**") uses an app listed in Annex 1 ("**App**") obtained through the Atlassian Marketplace
("**Agreement**"), including the end user agreement shown on the App's Marketplace listing. It is
between Customer and **Ben Yamazaki Bosworth**, an individual sole trader established in the
**United Kingdom** and trading as Bevelpine Apps ("**Bevelpine Apps**", "**we**"). If the DPA and the Agreement
conflict on the processing of personal data, the DPA prevails.

**This DPA applies only to the Apps listed in Annex 1**, which are Atlassian Marketplace apps built
on Atlassian's Forge platform. It does not apply to any other product or service of Bevelpine Apps,
including any Shopify app.

It applies automatically while Customer has an App installed. No signature is needed.

## 1. Definitions

"**Data Protection Laws**" means the laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data
under the Agreement. Depending on the case, that includes the EU General Data Protection
Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, and the California
Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("**CCPA**").

"**Customer Personal Data**" means the personal data the App processes on Customer's behalf, as
described in Annex 1.

"**Controller**", "**processor**", "**data subject**", "**personal data breach**" and
"**processing**" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "**Business**", "**service provider**",
"**sell**" and "**share**" have the meanings given in the CCPA.

"**Sub-processor**" means a third party we engage that processes Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and scope

2.1 For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the controller (under the CCPA, the business) and
Bevelpine Apps is the processor (under the CCPA, the service provider).

2.2 Each App runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, on Atlassian's infrastructure. Bevelpine Apps
operates no servers of its own for these Apps, and no App sends Customer Personal Data to
Bevelpine Apps or to any other destination outside Atlassian. The App's code processes the data
inside Atlassian's platform, for Customer's Atlassian site, as described in Annex 1.

2.3 **App logs.** Atlassian lets a site administrator share an App's logs with the App's developer.
The setting is shown as "Logs access" on the App's page under Connected apps. While it is on, we
can read up to 60 days of that App's log output for Customer's site. What those logs contain:
- **Our Apps' code writes no Customer Personal Data to the logs.** Label Colors for Jira,
  Initiative Timeline for Jira, User Access Audit for Jira, Bulk Excel & CSV Exporter for Jira and
  Read Receipts for Confluence write no log lines at all.
- **Hide from Export for Confluence** writes one line each time a page is exported. The line names
  the kinds of information Confluence passes to the App, not their contents. For example:
  `context keys: [ 'context', 'exportType', 'extensionPayload', 'contextToken' ]`.
- Each log line also carries a time and an invocation identifier generated by Atlassian.
- Atlassian also records, in the same logs, any error the App fails to handle while it runs. That
  covers both the server side and the App's page in the browser: an uncaught exception, an
  unhandled promise rejection or a `console.error`. None of our Apps' code calls `console.error`.
  The text of an error the App does not handle is not under our control and can appear in those
  logs.

Customer can turn "Logs access" off at any time. We use any log output only to support and fix
the App.

2.4 This DPA does not cover the relationship between Customer and Atlassian. Atlassian's own terms
and Data Processing Addendum govern that.

## 3. Processing on instructions

3.1 We process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. Those
instructions are: the Agreement; this DPA; and Customer's configuration and use of the App.
The only exception is where the law requires otherwise. In that case we will tell Customer
before processing, unless that law prohibits it.

3.2 We will tell Customer if we believe an instruction breaks Data Protection Laws.

3.3 Annex 1 sets out the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, and the
types of personal data and categories of data subjects.

## 4. Confidentiality

Everyone we authorise to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality.

## 5. Security

We maintain the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2. Their purpose is to
protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration,
unauthorised disclosure or access.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 Customer authorises the Sub-processor listed in Annex 3.

6.2 We will not add or replace a Sub-processor without first updating Annex 3 on this page, at
least 30 days before the change takes effect. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection
grounds within that period. If we cannot resolve the objection, Customer may stop using the
affected App and uninstall it.

6.3 Where we engage a Sub-processor, we will impose data-protection obligations on it that are
no less protective than this DPA, to the extent the Sub-processor's standard terms allow. We
remain responsible to Customer for its performance.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 **Data subject requests.** Every App keeps its data inside Customer's Atlassian site (Annex 1),
so Customer and Customer's administrators can act on most requests directly. If we receive a
request relating to Customer Personal Data, we will pass it to Customer and not answer it
ourselves unless Customer instructs us to. Taking into account the nature of the processing, we
will reasonably assist Customer in responding to data subject requests.

7.2 **Other assistance.** Taking into account the information available to us, we will reasonably
assist Customer with its obligations on:
- security;
- personal data breach notification;
- data protection impact assessments;
- prior consultation with a supervisory authority.

## 8. Personal data breaches

We will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach
affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice will contain the information then reasonably
available to us. We will notify the contact address Customer gives us, or the technical contact
Atlassian holds for the installation.

## 9. Deletion and return

9.1 Customer Personal Data stored by an App is held in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage for
Customer's installation. **When Customer uninstalls the App, that stored data is deleted by
Atlassian under Atlassian's data lifecycle for Forge hosted storage**, which first soft-deletes it
and then retains it for the rest of the retention period Atlassian sets out.

9.2 Where an App provides a way to delete stored records, Customer can delete them using the App
itself. Annex 1 states, for each App, what can be deleted and when.

9.3 Bevelpine Apps holds no separate copy that would need to be returned or deleted.

## 10. Audits and information

10.1 On written request, we will make available the information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA. That includes this DPA, the App's privacy policy, and the
answers in the App's Marketplace Privacy & Security tab.

10.2 Bevelpine Apps operates no infrastructure for these Apps. For the hosting environment, we
rely on Atlassian's own security reports and certifications. An audit or inspection under
Article 28(3)(h) GDPR is therefore limited to our own records and practices. It will be carried
out on reasonable notice, at Customer's cost, and no more than once a year unless a supervisory
authority requires otherwise.

## 11. International transfers

No App's code sends Customer Personal Data to Bevelpine Apps (section 2.2). The only route by which
App-related data reaches us is the App logs described in section 2.3, which are shared only if
Customer allows it and which our Apps' code keeps free of Customer Personal Data.
Unhandled error text, as section 2.3 describes, is not under our control and could in principle
contain personal data. Where that happens we use it only to diagnose and fix the App, and we keep
no copy of it.
Customer Personal Data is stored and processed on Atlassian's infrastructure. Where it is
located, and what transfer mechanisms apply, is governed by Atlassian's terms and Data Processing
Addendum and by Customer's data residency settings with Atlassian. If we ever need to receive
Customer Personal Data in a way that is a restricted transfer, we will first put in place an
appropriate transfer mechanism, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses
and the UK Addendum to them, and update this DPA.

## 12. CCPA service-provider terms

Where the CCPA applies, Bevelpine Apps:

- (a) processes Customer Personal Data only for the limited and specified business purpose of
  providing the App to Customer, as described in the Agreement and Annex 1;
- (b) will not **sell** or **share** Customer Personal Data;
- (c) will not retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than (a),
  including any commercial purpose other than providing the App, or outside the direct business
  relationship with Customer;
- (d) will not combine Customer Personal Data with personal information we receive from, or on
  behalf of, another person, or collect from our own interactions with a consumer, except as the
  CCPA permits for a service provider;
- (e) will comply with the CCPA's obligations that apply to it, and provide the same level of
  privacy protection the CCPA requires of Customer;
- (f) grants Customer the right to take reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure we use
  Customer Personal Data consistently with Customer's CCPA obligations, and to stop and remedy
  unauthorised use;
- (g) will notify Customer if we determine we can no longer meet our CCPA obligations;
- (h) will ensure any Sub-processor is bound by written terms no less protective than this
  section.

We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

## 13. Term, liability and changes

13.1 This DPA lasts as long as we process Customer Personal Data for Customer.

13.2 Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of the Agreement, to
the extent Data Protection Laws allow.

13.3 We may update this DPA to reflect changes in law or in the Apps. We will post the updated
version on this page with a new "Last updated" date. A change that reduces the protection given
to Customer Personal Data applies only after the 30-day notice period described in section 6.2.

## Annex 2 — Technical and organisational measures

- The Apps run only on Atlassian's Forge platform. Bevelpine Apps operates no servers, databases
  or third-party services for them.
- No App declares external network permissions (egress), so no App can send Customer Personal
  Data to Bevelpine Apps or to any third party. The only developer-facing channel is Atlassian's
  optional log sharing (section 2.3), and our Apps' code writes no Customer Personal Data to it.
- Stored data lives in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage, which Atlassian scopes to each App
  installation.
- No App declares permission to change Customer's Jira or Confluence data. Where an App reads
  that data, it reads it with the signed-in user's own permissions.
- Every App that stores data writes it only through its server-side code, which identifies the
  caller from Atlassian's authenticated context and applies the App's rules on who may make each
  change before writing. The other Apps store nothing.
- No analytics, tracking or cookies inside the Apps.
- Access to the developer accounts that can deploy the Apps is restricted to Bevelpine Apps.
